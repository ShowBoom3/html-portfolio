ShowBoom Recommends!

My Top 3 Movies of all-time.

How to Train your Dragon

Animated. Live Action was good, but the animated ones are better. Loved the story, the character development, and of course, the dragons.

Project Hail Mary

The bonding between Grace and Rocky really makes you want to have an alien friend that is both dumb and smart at the same time.

Your Name

The beautiful story telling using two people and the time between them is very unique, and both their perspectives are different yet quite similar.
